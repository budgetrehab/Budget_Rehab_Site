Delete your account & data

For the Budget Rehab app by Budget Rehab Limited, on the App Store and Google Play

HomePrivacySupport

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open Budget Rehab on your iPhone or Android phone.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Erase my data (or Delete account).
  4. Confirm. Your data is deleted straight away.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

If you've uninstalled the app or can't open it, send us a deletion request:

  1. Email admin@budgetrehab.co.uk with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Send it from the email address linked to your Budget Rehab account, or tell us that address.
  3. We'll confirm your identity and delete your account and data within 30 days, usually much sooner. We'll email you when it's done.
Email a deletion request

Uninstalling the app does not by itself delete data stored on our servers.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Bank statements and transactionsPermanently deleted
Credit report details and debtsPermanently deleted
Income, payslips and budget plansPermanently deleted
Your account and email addressPermanently deleted
Backup copiesCleared within 30 days

What we keep, and for how long

  • A record of your deletion request (date and email address only): kept for 2 years, to show we complied.
  • Support emails and complaint records: kept for up to 2 years, then deleted.
  • Payment records: payments are handled by Apple or Google, which keep their own records. We don't hold your card details.

Deletion can't be undone. You can delete only your financial data and keep your account by using "Erase my data" in the app. See our Privacy Policy for more.
