Getting started

What does Budget Rehab do?

Budget Rehab reads your bank statements and credit report, works out where your money goes, and builds a weekly or monthly budget. It also shows how long it will take you to become debt free, in weeks, days, months, years and payslips.

How do I set up my pay, debts and documents?

Everything is on one screen. Enter your pay and how often you're paid (weekly, fortnightly, four-weekly or monthly), add your debts, and import your credit report. Then pick a weekly or monthly plan.

Do I need to connect my bank account?

No. Budget Rehab never asks for your online banking login. You just upload PDF statements you've downloaded from your bank's app or website.

Statements & credit reports

How do I upload a bank statement?
  1. Download your statement as a PDF from your bank's app or website.
  2. Open the Spending tab in Budget Rehab.
  3. Tap to upload, choose the PDF, and tap Submit. You'll see a percentage as it uploads.
  4. Your submitted statements are listed so you can see which months you've added.
How many statements should I upload?

We recommend at least 3 months for an accurate average. More months give a better picture, especially if your spending changes from month to month.

My statement won't upload or reads incorrectly
  • Make sure it's an original PDF from your bank, not a photo or screenshot.
  • Password-protected PDFs can't be read. Download an unprotected copy.
  • Check you have a stable internet connection, then try again.
  • If it still fails, email us with your bank's name (not the statement) and we'll look into it.
Where do I get my credit report?

You can get your statutory credit report free from the UK credit reference agencies: Experian, Equifax and TransUnion. Download it as a PDF and import it on the Import screen. Budget Rehab uses the debts in the report, not your credit score.

Spending & budgets

How do I see where my money is going?

After submitting your statements, tap Generate on the Spending tab. You'll see your average spending per month by category, a breakdown by merchant, and your subscriptions shown separately so they're easy to spot.

A transaction is in the wrong category

Tap the transaction or merchant to change its category. Your budget updates straight away.

Can I switch between weekly and monthly budgets?

Yes. You can change between a weekly and a monthly plan at any time, and your figures will be recalculated.

How is my debt-free date worked out?

We use the debts you've added, your income and your spending to estimate how much you can put towards debt each pay period, and how long it will take to clear it. It's an estimate. It changes if your income, spending or debts change.

Your data & privacy

Is my data safe?

Your data is encrypted, never sold, never used for advertising and never used to train AI. Read our full Privacy Policy.

Why does the app ask permission before reading my statement?

Your documents are read by a trusted third-party AI service, so we ask before sending anything. You can change your mind at any time in Settings. If you switch it off, you can still type your figures in by hand. See how documents are read.

How do I erase my data or delete my account?

Go to Settings → Erase my data in the app, on iPhone or Android. This permanently deletes your statements, credit report, debts, income and plans, and it can't be undone. If you've uninstalled the app, you can request deletion online.

How do I make a data protection complaint?

Email admin@budgetrehab.co.uk with the subject "Data protection complaint". We'll acknowledge it within 30 days, investigate and tell you the outcome. You can also contact the ICO. Read our complaints process.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. Email admin@budgetrehab.co.uk and we'll send it to you within one month, free of charge.

Account & billing

How do I cancel a subscription or get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple or Google, not by us.

  • iPhone: open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Android: open the Google Play app, tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. For refunds, see Google Play refunds.

Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.

I've got a new phone

Download Budget Rehab from the App Store or Google Play and sign in with the same account. If your data doesn't appear, email us and we'll help.

Need help with debt?

Budget Rehab is a budgeting tool, not regulated financial or debt advice. If you're worried about debt, these UK services offer free, confidential help:

  • MoneyHelper: free, government-backed money and debt guidance
  • StepChange: free debt advice charity
  • National Debtline: free debt advice by phone and online
  • Citizens Advice: free help with money, benefits and more

If money worries are affecting your mental health, you can talk to Samaritans any time, free, on 116 123.
