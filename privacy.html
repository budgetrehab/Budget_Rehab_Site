1. Who we are

Budget Rehab is a budgeting app that helps people in the UK understand their spending, manage debt and work out how long it will take to become debt free.

The data controller responsible for your personal information is Budget Rehab Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17557911), registered office 66 Paul Street, London, England, United Kingdom, EC2A 4NA ("Budget Rehab", "we", "us").

Our privacy contact is Natasha Chipampe, who can be reached at admin@budgetrehab.co.uk.

3. How we use your information

  • To read your statements and sort your spending into categories, subscriptions and merchants.
  • To calculate your weekly or monthly budget plan.
  • To estimate how long it will take you to become debt free, shown in weeks, days, months, years and payslips.
  • To answer your support requests and complaints.
  • To send early-access and launch updates, only if you joined the waitlist.
  • To keep the app secure, prevent misuse and fix bugs.

We only use your data for the purposes above. We do not sell it, rent it, use it for advertising, use it to train AI models, or share it with lenders, debt collectors or credit reference agencies. We do not use it to make credit or lending decisions.

5. How your documents are read

When you upload a statement, credit report or payslip, it is sent over an encrypted connection to Cloudflare, a third-party service whose AI (Cloudflare Workers AI) reads the document and extracts your transactions for us.

We ask before we share. Before your first upload, the app clearly tells you that your document will be sent to a third-party AI service, and asks for your permission. Nothing is sent unless you agree. You can withdraw permission at any time in the app's settings. If you do, you can still enter your figures by hand, but statement reading will be switched off.

The AI provider:

  • processes your documents only on our instructions and only to extract your financial information
  • does not use your documents to train its AI models
  • is bound by a data processing agreement and must protect your data at least as well as we do
  • does not keep your document after reading it. Cloudflare only stores content that we choose to save in our own storage, and we delete original uploaded files within 28 days

6. Automated calculations

Your spending categories, budget plan and debt-free estimate are worked out automatically from the information you provide. These calculations are guidance only. They do not lead to any decision that has a legal or similarly significant effect on you, such as a credit decision. You can review, edit or remove any figure or category at any time, and you can contact us if you think something has been worked out wrongly.

7. Who we share your information with

We only share information with service providers that help us run Budget Rehab. Each one is under a contract that requires it to keep your data secure, confidential and protected to the same standard as this policy, and to use it only on our instructions.

The types of providers we share information with are:

  • Cloud hosting and storage providers, to run the app and store your data securely
  • AI document-reading providers, to read the statements, credit reports and payslips you upload (only with your permission, see section 5)
  • App support tools, such as crash and error reporting, to fix problems
  • Email and sign-up services, to manage our early-access list and reply to your messages
  • App stores (Apple and Google), which handle downloads and any in-app payments under their own privacy policies

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect the rights and safety of our users, or if Budget Rehab is reorganised or sold, in which case your information would stay protected by this policy. We will never sell your personal information.

8. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the UK, for example in the United States. Where that happens, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, the ICO's International Data Transfer Agreement, or the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses. You can ask us for details.

10. Deleting your data and account

You're in control. You can delete your data at any time, for free:

  • In the app: go to Settings → Erase my data or Delete account. This works on both iPhone and Android.
  • Without the app: visit budgetrehab.co.uk/delete-account, or email admin@budgetrehab.co.uk with the subject "Delete my data".

Deletion permanently removes your statements, transactions, credit report details, debts, income, budget plans and account. It cannot be undone. We only keep information we're legally required to keep (for example, a record that a complaint was handled), and only for as long as the law requires.

Deleting the app from your phone does not, on its own, delete data stored on our servers. Please use one of the options above.

11. Keeping your data secure

  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.
  • Access is limited to the people who need it to run the service, and protected by multi-factor authentication.
  • We assess the risks of how we handle financial data in a data protection impact assessment (DPIA), and review it when the app changes.
  • We never ask for your banking login, so we can't access or move your money.

No system is 100% secure. If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we will report it to the ICO within 72 hours where required, and tell you without undue delay.

12. Emails and marketing

We only send marketing or launch emails if you've opted in, for example by joining our early-access list. Every email has an unsubscribe link, or you can email us to stop. Service messages, such as replies to your support requests, aren't marketing and don't need opt-in.

13. Our website and cookies

budgetrehab.co.uk is a simple website. We use no advertising or tracking cookies. If you join the early-access list, we collect your name, email address and phone number. We use your phone number only to contact you about your early access or launch if email doesn't reach you. We never use it for cold calls or share it, and we don't send marketing texts unless you separately agree.

14. App permissions

Budget Rehab only asks for access to the files you choose to upload, using your phone's standard file picker. If you allow notifications, we'll send budget reminders. You can turn these off in your phone's settings. We don't ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo library.

15. Budget Rehab is not financial advice

Budget Rehab is a budgeting tool. It gives general guidance based on the information you provide. It is not regulated financial advice or debt advice, and it does not recommend debt solutions such as IVAs, debt management plans or bankruptcy. If you're struggling with debt, free and confidential help is available from MoneyHelper, StepChange, National Debtline and Citizens Advice.

16. Age

Budget Rehab is for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a child has given us their information, contact us and we'll delete it promptly.

17. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • access a copy of your personal information
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • erase your information
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • receive your data in a portable format
  • withdraw consent at any time, for example to AI document reading or marketing emails, without affecting anything done before
  • complain to us and to the ICO (see below)

To use any of these rights, email admin@budgetrehab.co.uk. It's free. We'll respond within one month, and we'll tell you if we need longer for a complex request. We may ask you to confirm your identity first, to protect your data.

18. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your personal information, please tell us so we can put it right. Our process:

  1. Contact us at admin@budgetrehab.co.uk with the subject "Data protection complaint". Tell us what happened and what you'd like us to do. You can also use the support form in the app.
  2. We'll acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, usually much sooner.
  3. We'll investigate without undue delay, and may ask you for more information.
  4. We'll tell you the outcome, what we found and any action we've taken.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113. The ICO usually expects you to raise the complaint with us first.

19. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add features. If we make important changes, especially to how we share your data, we'll tell you in the app or by email before they take effect, and ask for your permission again where needed. The date at the top shows the latest version.
